Sourcing is by **theme, not system** — see [library.md](library.md) for the full
collection and [warlock-rules.md](warlock-rules.md) for the target ruleset.

## Magazine & fanzine archives — bulk short-form

Hundreds of 4-10 page adventures, NPC write-ups and setting colour. **The richest
seam for single-session play**, and the least prone to world-saving escalation.

- **White Dwarf** — 218 PDFs, 7511 MB. Full run. Early issues are dense with WFRP/40k scenarios and NPCs.
- **Warpstone** — 19 PDFs, 458 MB. The dedicated WFRP fanzine — arguably the best WFRP writing outside the core line.
- **Strike to Stun** — 125 PDFs, 25 MB. Fan compilation; individual articles as separate PDFs, so already scenario-sized.
- **Legion** — 10 PDFs, 9 MB. Small fanzine run.
- **Liber Fanatica** — 7 PDFs, 78 MB. High-quality fan supplements, several with adventures.

## Campaigns — structural reference

Mostly the *wrong shape* for Wyrd (published campaigns escalate by design), but worth
mining for structure, NPCs and locations rather than running as written.

- Death On The Reik — `195 MB`
- The Power Behind The Throne — `26 MB`
- Something Rotten in Kislev — `36 MB`

Also present standalone at v1 root: `02 - The Enemy Within.pdf`, `04 - Shadows Over
Bögenhafen.pdf`, `05 - Death On The Reik.pdf`, `06 - The Power Behind The Throne.pdf`,
`08 - Something Rotten in Kislev.pdf`, `11 - Empire In Flames.pdf`.

The definitive Warhammer meta-campaign. Note that even across eight parts the PCs are
never the Empire's saviours — they are pawns who survive. That restraint is the model.

## Solo & GM-emulation prior art

Not scenarios — technique. How to run one player, pace without a group, and let an
oracle say no.

- **Mythic** (2 PDFs) — the GM Emulator. Scene-by-scene pacing, the "is it as expected?"
  check, and the discipline that stops solo play collapsing into wish-fulfilment.
- **Scarlet Heroes** (4 PDFs) — explicitly built for **one PC**, with damage-scaling so a
  single character survives content written for a party. Directly relevant.
- **Sellswords and Spellslingers**, **Rangers of Shadow Deep**, **Four Against Darkness**
  — solo skirmish with NPC companions; useful for how they handle companion agency.
- **Destiny Quest**, **Lone Wolf Adventure Game**, **Fighting Fantasy** — gamebook pacing;
  relevant to the beat structure of a twenty-minute session.
