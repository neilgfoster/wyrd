# Recap — end of session 1

**Wendel Kastner**, initiate of Verena, Hemmelfurt on the Reik. 1 Pflugzeit 2512.

He woke on the vestry floor with no memory after the tenth hour and the taste of cheap
Bögenhafen red. The shrine's ledger — which in a town of nine hundred souls *is* the law —
lies open, and a page has been cut from it, cleanly, with a blade. The entries either side
are dated nine days ago. His key was on his belt the whole time.

He tried to remember the night and could not, but one fragment surfaced: the groan of the
shrine door's lower hinge. He came back here during the missing hours.

He told Brida Voss the truth, and told it badly — out of order, defensively, like a man
laying groundwork. She has kept this ledger twenty-two years and she has buried a husband
and two sons, and she told him she would have helped a frightened man. She unbolted the
outer door to make a point, and will not be alone in the room with him again until she knows
what was on that page.
